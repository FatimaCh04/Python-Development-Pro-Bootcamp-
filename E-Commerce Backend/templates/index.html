Our Story

We build gear like it has to survive us.

FIELD & FORM started in a two-car garage with one sewing machine and a pile of army-surplus canvas. Ten years on, we still cut every pattern the same way — by hand, in small runs, tested by the people who make it before it ever reaches a shelf.

No planned obsolescence. No seasonal landfill drops. Just equipment built to be handed down.
