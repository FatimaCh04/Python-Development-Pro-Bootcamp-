UNDER THE HOOD

Symmetric encryption, explained plainly.

Fernet generates one secret key from your master password. That key both locks and unlocks every record — lose it, and the vault stays sealed even from you. That's the trade you're making: total privacy for total responsibility.

  • Ciphertext is authenticated — tampered records are rejected, not silently corrupted.
  • Each entry is encrypted independently, so a leaked record never exposes the rest.
  • The vault file is portable JSON — back it up, move it, it stays unreadable without the key.
