How It Works

From input to ML-driven estimate in three steps

1

Enter Details

Provide the property's area in sq ft, bedrooms, bathrooms, and select the neighbourhood from the trained dataset.

2

Model Inference

Your inputs go to the Flask API, which runs them through the saved Random Forest pipeline — StandardScaler + OneHotEncoder + RandomForestRegressor.

3

Get Estimate

The predicted price in PKR comes directly from the Joblib-saved model. No formula, no hardcoding, pure ML inference.
